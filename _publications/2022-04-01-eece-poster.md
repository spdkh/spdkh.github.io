Poster presentation (awarded 2nd place) at the University of Memphis Herff College of Engineering 8th Annual ECE Department Poster Competition. The poster surveys physics-guided deep learning methodologies for computational imaging inverse problems, with a focus on structured illumination microscopy (SIM). Topics covered include unrolled optimization networks, physics-informed generative adversarial networks, and channel-attention architectures that incorporate optical forward models. The work contextualizes ongoing research on UPIGAN and related SIM reconstruction frameworks within the broader landscape of model-based deep learning for imaging science.
