Extended Team Description Paper for the Parsian team (Amirkabir University of Technology) competing in the RoboCup Small Size Soccer League Senior division at RoboCup 2016 in Leipzig, Germany. The paper documents the team's hardware and software developments including robot electronics, multi-agent coordination strategies, motion planning, and communication systems. Contributions include DC-DC power conversion circuitry, wireless NRF communication links, and FPGA-based soft-core processor implementations in Verilog. The Parsian team is one of approximately 20 elite international university teams in the SSL Senior League.
