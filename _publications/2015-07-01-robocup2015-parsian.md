Team Description Paper for the Parsian team (Amirkabir University of Technology) at RoboCup 2015 in Hefei, China, Small Size Soccer League Senior division. The paper describes the team's autonomous multi-robot soccer platform including robot mechanical design, electronics, inter-robot communication, and AI-based game strategy. The SSL requires fully autonomous robots (max. 18 cm diameter) operating under off-board vision with on-board computation for locomotion and kicking, coordinated through a centralized AI referee.
