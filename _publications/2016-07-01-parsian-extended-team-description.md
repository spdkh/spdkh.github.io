Extended Team Description paper for the **Parsian** Small Size League team at RoboCup 2016 (Leipzig). The Parsian SSL team is one of the longstanding research and competition platforms at Amirkabir University of Technology. My contributions covered electronics design (boost DC-DC conversion, two-way NRF wireless link), Verilog FPGA implementation of a soft-core microprocessor, and integration testing.

Recommended citation: MM. Rahimi, MM. Shirazi, **S. P. Dajkhosh**, A. Zolanvari, M. Arfaee, H. Kazemi Khoshkijari, AH. Abbassi Fashami, A. Saeidi, MA. Khosravi. (2016). "Parsian Extended Team Description." *RoboCup International Symposium*, Leipzig, Germany.
