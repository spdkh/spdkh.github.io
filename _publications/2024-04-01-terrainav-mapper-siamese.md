A core piece of the **TerrAInav** vision-based UAV-navigation stack. We train a **Siamese network** to produce robust embeddings of aerial-image patches, embeddings that survive variations in lighting, weather, season, and viewpoint. These embeddings power downstream geo-localization and visual SLAM in the broader TerrAInav pipeline.

Joint work with the Jacobs Lab at the University of Memphis. Manuscript in preparation for the *Journal of Applied Remote Sensing (JARS)*.
