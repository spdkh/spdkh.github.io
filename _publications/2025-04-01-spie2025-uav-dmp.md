Autonomous UAV navigation through tree canopies poses significant challenges due to occlusion, variable lighting, and GPS-signal degradation. This paper presents a Dynamic Motion Primitive (DMP)-based framework for adaptive path planning that enables a UAV to navigate below forest canopies during environmental stream data collection. The approach encodes motion trajectories as learnable DMP parameters, allowing the system to generalize previously demonstrated flight patterns to new environments while adapting to obstacles. Experimental results validate the framework's ability to collect stream-assessment data in challenging, partially occluded field conditions.
