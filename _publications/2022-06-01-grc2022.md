Poster presentation at the International Gordon Research Conference on Image Science. We present a quantitative performance evaluation of a physics-informed channel-attention generative adversarial network for 3D image reconstruction in Structured Illumination Microscopy. The network incorporates the SIM forward model and optical transfer function into the channel-attention mechanism, guiding the GAN to invert known physics during reconstruction. Experiments on real 3D SIM volumetric datasets demonstrate improved super-resolution fidelity compared to standard deep learning baselines, with metrics evaluated on both simulated ground truth and real biological specimens.
