This paper presents a vision-based uncrewed aerial system designed for real-time assessment of environmental streams. The system integrates deep learning-based perception with a UAV platform to enable autonomous water-body detection, stream condition monitoring, and field data collection through challenging environments including tree canopies. The approach is evaluated against field-collected datasets and demonstrated as part of the SICK LiDAR StreamEye Multi-UAS Visual Stream Assessment Challenge. Results highlight the system's capability for scalable, real-time environmental monitoring in GPS-constrained conditions.
