TerrAInaVision presents a systematic benchmark of aerial imaging and perception pipelines for UAV-based environmental stream assessment. The work evaluates multimodal approaches including visible-band imaging, feature extraction, and detection architectures against standardized field-collected datasets. The benchmark provides performance metrics and analysis to guide the development of robust vision-AI systems for environmental monitoring applications. Manuscript in preparation.
