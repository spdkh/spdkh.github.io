Joint work between the **CIRL group at the University of Memphis** and the **Optics Department at Universitat de València**. We characterize how the *spatial coherence properties* of a finite-extent (extended) illumination source shape the axial modulation depth of the structured pattern, and therefore the optical sectioning, depth-of-field, and 3D reconstruction quality of a SIM system.

The key contribution is a model that lets system designers trade off source extent, modulation depth, and out-of-focus rejection in a principled way, rather than tuning them empirically. Published in **Optics Express**, vol. 31, issue 22, pp. 36568--36589, November 2023.
