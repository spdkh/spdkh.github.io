**TerrAInav Sim** is the open-source companion to the TerrAInav navigation stack. It synthesizes UAV-perspective aerial imagery from publicly available satellite data, turning expensive, hard-to-replicate flight collection into a reproducible benchmark anyone can run.

The simulator covers viewpoint, lighting, and seasonal variation, and exposes hooks for plugging in custom DNN-based perception models. It's intended as a community resource for vision-based-navigation, geo-localization, and aerial-image-translation research.

Published in **Remote Sensing, MDPI**, April 2025 (vol. 17, no. 8, article 1454). DOI: [10.3390/rs17081454](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs17081454)
