Team description paper for the **AUT-Parsian** entry in the 2014 RoboCup Small Size League (Brazil). My contributions covered the on-board electronics, boost DC-DC conversion (16.8 V → 200 V for kicker capacitors), two-way NRF wireless link between the robots and the central computer, and an FPGA-hosted soft-core microprocessor implementation in Verilog.

Recommended citation: A. Saeidi, MH. Malmir, MM. Shirazi, M. Behbooei, S. Boluki, M. Kazemi, S. M. Mohaimanian Pour, P. Ghanbari, S. Jamshidiha, **S. P. Dajkhosh**, A. Zahedi. (2014). "AUT-Parsian Team Description." *RoboCup International Symposium*, João Pessoa, Brazil.
