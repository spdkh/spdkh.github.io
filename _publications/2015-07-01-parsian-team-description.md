Team description paper for the **Parsian** entry in the 2015 RoboCup Small Size League (Hefei, China). My contributions to this iteration centered on the wireless link, FPGA microprocessor, and contributions to motor-controller and processor-board electronics.

Recommended citation: A. Zolanvari, MM. Shirazi, **S. P. Dajkhosh**, AM. Naderi, M. Arfaee, M. Behbooei, H. Kazemi Khoshkijari, E. Tazimi, MM. Rahimi, A. Saeidi Shahrivar. (2015). "Parsian Team Description." *RoboCup International Symposium*, Hefei, China.
