TerrAInav Mapper introduces a Siamese network approach for robust aerial image feature extraction targeting UAV geo-localization and terrain mapping. The architecture learns discriminative cross-view feature embeddings between simulated and real aerial imagery, enabling reliable place recognition for vision-based navigation in GPS-denied environments. Applications include drone-based environmental monitoring and autonomous navigation over varying terrain. Manuscript in preparation.
