This paper presents a deep neural network framework for aerial image feature extraction and cross-view mapping to support UAV geo-localization and terrain navigation. The approach learns domain-invariant feature representations from simulated and real aerial imagery, enabling robust matching across viewpoint and illumination changes. The resulting feature maps support downstream navigation tasks including place recognition and map alignment for vision-based UAV localization in GPS-degraded environments. Performance is evaluated on multi-altitude aerial image datasets with varying environmental conditions.
