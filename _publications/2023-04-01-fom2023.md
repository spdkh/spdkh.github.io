This work presents a systematic comparison of two structured illumination microscopy (SIM) configurations: a tunable Wollaston-prism-based system and a three-wave interference SIM system. Using MATLAB-based simulation of optical transfer functions (OTFs), the study evaluates wide-field, tunable, and three-wave SIM geometries in terms of 3D resolution enhancement and axial modulation depth. The results inform optical system design choices for next-generation SIM implementations targeting live-cell and volumetric biological imaging. Collaborative work between the Computational Imaging Research Lab (University of Memphis) and the University of Valencia.
