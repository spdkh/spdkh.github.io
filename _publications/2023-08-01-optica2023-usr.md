We present USR (Unrolled Super-Resolution), a physics-aware deep learning framework for 3D image reconstruction in Structured Illumination Microscopy (SIM). USR unfolds the SIM inverse problem into a fixed number of trainable network stages, each corresponding to one iteration of a proximal optimization algorithm, with learned deep priors replacing hand-crafted regularizers. The SIM forward model and optical transfer function (OTF) are embedded directly into the unrolled architecture, constraining the reconstruction to physically realizable solutions. On real 3D SIM datasets, USR achieves higher-fidelity super-resolution reconstructions compared to conventional and channel-attention-based baselines, with improved artifact suppression and structural detail recovery.
