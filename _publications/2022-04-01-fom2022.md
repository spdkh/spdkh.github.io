This contribution investigates the use of spatially incoherent illumination sources with arbitrary shapes in structured illumination microscopy (SIM). The study develops analytical models and MATLAB simulations to characterize how source geometry modifies the effective optical transfer function and the achievable 3D super-resolution enhancement. Results show that realistic extended and non-uniform sources must be accounted for in system modeling to avoid reconstruction errors, and that tailored source shapes can be leveraged to optimize OTF coverage. This work informs more accurate SIM system design and calibration procedures.
