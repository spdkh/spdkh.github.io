This project, developed during the Neuromatch Academy 3.0 Computational Neuroscience Summer School, presents a machine learning pipeline for classifying Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) from resting-state functional MRI (fMRI) data. The pipeline applies dimensionality reduction techniques including PCA and ICA to high-dimensional fMRI connectivity features, followed by classification using standard ML algorithms. The study investigates which brain connectivity patterns are most discriminative for ASD classification and evaluates robustness across the ABIDE dataset. The work contributed to team learning goals in applied computational neuroscience and neuroimaging analysis.
