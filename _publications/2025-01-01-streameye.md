StreamEye addresses the challenge of scalable environmental stream monitoring using unmanned aerial vehicles. The system integrates real-time visual perception with multi-sensor aerial data collection through tree canopies to perform automated water-body detection, stream mapping, and environmental condition assessment. The proposed pipeline combines deep learning-based object detection with UAV-specific flight planning to enable robust monitoring in occluded and GPS-challenging field environments.
