We design and implement an approach for three-dimensional structured illumination microscopy (SIM) based on a quasi-monochromatic extended source illuminating a Wollaston prism. We demonstrate through analytical and experimental verification that a simple and accurate determination of the axial modulation of the SI pattern is achieved, enabling realistic characterization of the system's effective optical transfer function (OTF). System performance as a function of the extended source size is investigated with simulations. The results provide a practical framework for managing illumination-pattern modulation depth in Wollaston-based SIM systems and support accurate 3D image reconstruction in computational microscopy pipelines.
