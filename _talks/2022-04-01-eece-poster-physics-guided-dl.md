**Awarded 2nd Place.** A literature-review poster mapping the landscape of physics-guided deep learning for computational imaging, categorized by where the physics enters the network: input encoding, layer-level constraints, unrolled optimization, or loss-function regularization. Presented practical recommendations for SIM and related inverse problems.

Co-authors: **S. P. Dajkhosh**, C. Van, C. Preza.
