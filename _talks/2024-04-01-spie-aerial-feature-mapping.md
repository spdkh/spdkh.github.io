**Presenter.** Conference talk introducing the **TerrAInav Mapper** approach: training Siamese networks to produce robust, lighting-and-season-invariant embeddings of aerial image patches, and using those embeddings to ground UAV geo-localization.

Co-authors: **S. P. Dajkhosh**, O. Furxhi, C. K. Renshaw, E. Jacobs.
