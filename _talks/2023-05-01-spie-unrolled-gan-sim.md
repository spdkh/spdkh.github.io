**Presenter.** Talk accompanying the SPIE conference paper. I presented our **unrolled GAN** approach to SIM reconstruction, embedding the SIM forward model and OTF directly into the layers of the network so the GAN learns to *invert known physics* rather than re-discover it from data alone, and walked through quantitative results on real SIM datasets.

See the [associated paper](/publication/2023-05-image-reconstruction-sim-unrolled-gan) for details.
