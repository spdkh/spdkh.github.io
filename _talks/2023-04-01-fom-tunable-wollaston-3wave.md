Co-author. A side-by-side performance comparison between two structured-illumination architectures: a *tunable Wollaston-prism-based* SIM and a *three-wave interference* SIM. We evaluate trade-offs in modulation depth, axial sectioning, and reconstruction quality on matched samples.

Co-authors: A. Gimeno-Gomez, **S. P. Dajkhosh**, M. S. H. Younis, C. T. S. Van, C. Preza, J. C. Barreiro-Hervas, G. Saavedra.
