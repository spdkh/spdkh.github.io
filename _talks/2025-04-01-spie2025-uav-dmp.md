**Presenter.** Conference talk presenting a **Dynamic Motion Primitive (DMP)**-based path planning framework for UAV navigation through tree canopies during environmental stream assessment missions.

The work addresses a fundamental field challenge: GPS-degraded environments beneath dense forest canopy, where standard waypoint navigation fails. DMPs encode flight trajectories as learnable dynamical systems, allowing the UAV to generalize demonstrated paths to new terrain while adapting around obstacles.

Co-authors: **S. P. Dajkhosh**, D. G. Ortega, M. Davoodi, E. L. Jacobs.

Part of the **TerrAInav** project on vision-AI-based UAV navigation for environmental monitoring.
