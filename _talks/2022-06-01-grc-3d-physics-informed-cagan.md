Poster presentation. We extended channel-attention GANs (caGAN) with physics-aware components, OTF-aware data-consistency layers and SIM-pattern-aware feature gating, and benchmarked against vanilla caGAN baselines on 3D SIM datasets at the Gordon Research Conference.

Co-authors: **S. P. Dajkhosh**, C. Van, C. Preza.
