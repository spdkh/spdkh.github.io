Poster presentation of **USR** (Unrolled Super-Resolution) at OPTICA / Frontiers in Optics, Boston. USR plugs deep image priors into the SIM inversion via algorithm unrolling, combining the data-consistency guarantees of model-based reconstruction with the expressive priors of modern deep networks.

See the [associated paper](/publication/2023-08-usr-unrolled-super-resolution-sim) for details.
