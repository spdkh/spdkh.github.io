Co-author. Joint UoM × Universitat de València work. We analyze how a spatially incoherent illumination source of arbitrary shape modifies the SIM modulation transfer characteristic, and the implications for sectioning and 3D reconstruction.

Co-authors: A. Gimeno-Gomez, C. T. S. Van, **S. P. Dajkhosh**, C. Preza, J. C. Barreiro-Hervas, G. Saavedra.
