Presenter. A hands-on Python workshop introducing **knowledge-based** (a.k.a. physics-informed) deep learning: when and why to bake known physics into network architectures, and how to do it concretely with PyTorch and Keras. Walked through SIM imaging as a worked example.

Co-authors: **S. P. Dajkhosh**, C. Van, C. Preza.
