**Ph.D. research · Remote Sensing Lab (RSL), University of Memphis.**
**Role:** Lead developer and first author.

Collecting labelled aerial imagery for every flight path, altitude, and camera setting is slow and expensive. **TerrAInav Sim** generates UAV aerial imagery from satellite data instead, giving controllable, repeatable flights for training and benchmarking vision-based navigation and geo-localization models.

- Simulates UAV camera views along arbitrary flight paths from satellite imagery.
- Released as open source so other groups can reproduce and extend the experiments.
- Used as the data and evaluation backbone of the [TerrAInav](/portfolio/02-uav-vision-navigation/) navigation work.

**Stack:** Python, OpenCV, satellite / geospatial imagery.

**Publication:** S. P. Dajkhosh, O. Furxhi, P. M. Le, E. L. Jacobs, *TerrAInav Sim: An Open-Source Simulation of UAV Aerial Imaging from Satellite Data*, **Remote Sensing (MDPI)**, 2025 ([doi](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs17081454)).
