**Undergraduate research · Parsian Robotics Lab, Amirkabir University of Technology · 2013–2016.**
**Role:** Electronics team lead. I led the 3–4 person electronics sub-team inside the 10–15 person Parsian team, competing in the international RoboCup Small Size League in João Pessoa (2014), Hefei (2015), and Leipzig (2016).

**Small Size League robots**
- **High-voltage power:** designed a **boost DC-DC converter** stepping 16.8 V up to 200 V for the kicker capacitor stage.
- **Wireless:** designed a **two-way NRF wireless link** between the central computer and the robot fleet.
- **FPGA:** implemented a **soft-core microprocessor** on the robot's FPGA in Verilog (Xilinx ISE).

**MIROSOT robot, AUTCUP Senior League (2016)**
- Designed and implemented the full embedded hardware: an FPGA processor board (Verilog / Xilinx), an ARM Cortex-M4 motor controller, motor-driver circuits, and inter-board communication.

**Quad-copter internship**
- Designed a fully integrated autonomous flight board: processor, motor drivers, IMU, NRF link, and vision. Advisor: Dr. M. Khosravi.

**Outputs:** RoboCup team description papers, including *Parsian Extended Team Description* (RoboCup 2016, Leipzig). See [Publications](/publications/).
