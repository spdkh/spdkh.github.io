**Undergraduate research · Parsian Robotics Lab, Amirkabir University · 2013–2016.**

A long stint of hands-on electronics and embedded design for the **Parsian** Small Size soccer-robot team, competing internationally at RoboCup Brazil (2014), China (2015), and Germany (2016).

- **High-voltage power:** designed a **boost DC-DC converter** stepping 16.8 V → 200 V for the kicker capacitor stage.
- **Wireless:** designed a **two-way NRF wireless link** between the central computer and the robot fleet.
- **FPGA:** **full Verilog implementation of a soft-core microprocessor** running inside the robot's FPGA (Xilinx ISE flow).
- **Mechatronics:** designed a fully integrated autonomous flight board for the team's quad-copter project, processor, motor drivers, IMU, NRF link, and analog camera vision.

Three RoboCup Symposium team-description papers (2014, 2015, 2016), see [Publications](/publications/).
