**Farzanegan 1 (NODET), Tehran · Jul 2013 – May 2021.**
**Role:** Programming & robotics teacher, and mentor for students' year-end projects.

After almost a year of learning and practicing programming, in both the compulsory programming classes and a volunteer programming research class, my students built a project for **"Sunshine"**, the exhibition Farzanegan holds at the end of each year. For each project the students:

- presented their work with a **PowerPoint** presentation, using the presentation techniques we practiced in class,
- designed a **poster** in Photoshop, and
- wrote a short **thesis** on the project in Microsoft Word.

### Highlight

**Neural-network asteroid classifier** (Python 3: TensorFlow, Keras, Pandas): won **2nd place in national and international competitions**.
