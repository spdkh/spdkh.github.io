**BSc thesis (graded A+) · CMVS Lab, Amirkabir University of Technology · 2017–2018.**

Closed-loop autonomous indoor landing of a quad-copter on a fiducial-marked pad, entirely on-board, no external positioning system.

- **Vision:** Raspberry Pi running OpenCV with **ARUCO markers** for pose estimation of the landing pad in the camera frame, in Python and C++.
- **Control:** independent **PID controllers** for each degree of freedom of the airframe.
- **Toolchain:** integrated the C++ vision code into MATLAB / Simulink for offline analysis and tuning.

Advisor: Dr. F. Abdollahi.
