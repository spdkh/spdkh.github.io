**Ph.D. research (earlier project) · Remote Sensing Lab (RSL), University of Memphis.**
**Role:** Lead researcher and first author. Advisor: Dr. E. Jacobs.

TerrAInav was the first part of my Ph.D.: helping a UAV plan its path and localize itself from what its camera sees, in the places where environmental data is collected, such as along streams and under tree canopies. My current dissertation work, [Anaita](/portfolio/07-streameye-stream-assessment/), builds on it.

- **Path planning under canopies:** **Dynamic Movement Primitives (DMPs)** that generate smooth, adaptable trajectories for collecting stream data through tree canopies.
- **Aerial image registration and geo-localization:** deep networks (TerrAInav Mapper, Siamese networks) that match UAV frames against satellite / reference imagery.
- **Field data:** real-time water-body mapping and multi-sensor aerial data collection.
- **Simulation:** [TerrAInav Sim](/portfolio/06-terrainav-sim/), our open-source aerial imaging simulator.

**Stack:** Python (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Ultralytics, OpenCV), MATLAB, embedded systems, electrical circuit design.
