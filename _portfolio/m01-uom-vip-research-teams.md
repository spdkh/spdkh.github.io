**University of Memphis · Sep 2022 – present.**
**Role:** Mentor, in collaboration with Dr. S. Ivey and Dr. E. Jacobs.

In the VIP program, undergraduates earn credit for contributing to active faculty research. I mentor students individually and in teams of 4–6, guiding them from the first literature search to working hardware and results.

**What I mentor students on**
- Research methodology, and reading and writing papers.
- Programming in Python and MATLAB.
- Electronic circuit design.
- 3D mechanical design and fabrication.
