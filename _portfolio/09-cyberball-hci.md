**Institute for Cognitive & Brain Sciences, Shahid Beheshti University, Tehran · 2018–2019.**
**Role:** Developer (volunteer research collaborator) under Dr. Kh. Borhani.

Built a Python human-computer interface that runs two classic behavioral experiments back to back:

- **Cyberball:** a virtual ball-tossing game used to induce a feeling of social exclusion.
- **Iowa Gambling Task:** a card-selection task that measures risky decision-making.

The software ran the sessions and logged participant responses so the lab could study the effect of social rejection on risk-taking.

**Stack:** Python.
