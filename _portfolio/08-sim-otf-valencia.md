**M.Sc. research · Computational Imaging Research Lab (CIRL), University of Memphis, with the University of Valencia.**
**Role:** Second author, in a five-person collaboration; I ran the OTF simulations in MATLAB.

Structured Illumination Microscopy (SIM) resolution depends on how the illumination pattern is generated. I simulated and compared the **optical transfer functions** of wide-field, tunable (Wollaston-based), and three-wave SIM geometries, including how an extended, spatially incoherent light source changes the axial modulation of the pattern.

**Stack:** MATLAB, optical-transfer-function modeling, structured illumination microscopy.

**Outputs**
- A. Gimeno-Gomez, **S. P. Dajkhosh**, et al., *Management of the axial modulation of the illumination pattern in structured illumination microscopy using an extended illumination source*, **Optics Express**, 2023 ([doi](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.499556)).
- Focus on Microscopy 2022 and 2023 talks.
