**Aug 2024 – May 2025** &middot; Two semesters (Fall 2024, Spring 2025).

Sole instructor of record for **EECE 2207: Engineering Mathematics Applications**, an undergraduate course introducing MATLAB and its applications in engineering mathematics.

**Topics covered:**

- MATLAB fundamentals: scripts, functions, data types, plotting
- Numerical methods: root-finding, numerical integration, ODE solvers
- Linear algebra applications: matrix operations, eigenvalue problems
- Signal processing basics and visualizations
- Engineering problem-solving workflows in MATLAB

**Course design responsibilities:**

- Developed and delivered lectures independently
- Created assignments, quizzes, and exams aligned with engineering math outcomes
- Held office hours and provided one-on-one student support

This course serves students across all ECE concentrations and reinforces mathematical foundations with hands-on computational tools.
