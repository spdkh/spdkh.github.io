**Jul – Aug 2021.** Teaching Assistant for two consecutive **Neuromatch Academy** summer schools.

**Computational Neuroscience School (July).** Helped students cooperate on hands-on Python exercises (NumPy, SciPy, PyTorch) for projects in computational neuroscience. Course material is available [here](https://compneuro.neuromatch.io/).

**Deep Learning School (August).** Helped students implement deep-learning algorithms in PyTorch, Numpy, and SciPy across the standard Neuromatch curriculum.

Both schools serve thousands of international students; TAs work directly with small pods of 4–8 throughout each day.
