**Aug 2022 – present** &middot; Continual, in collaboration with **Dr. S. Ivey** and **Dr. E. Jacobs**.

I mentor undergraduates individually and in teams of 4--6 through the University of Memphis Virtually Integrated Program (VIP), where students earn credit for contributing to active faculty research.

**What I cover with mentees:**

- **Research methodology:** framing research questions, structuring experiments, reading and synthesizing papers, writing clearly.
- **Programming for research:** Python (NumPy, SciPy, PyTorch, OpenCV) and MATLAB workflows tailored to each student's project.
- **Electronics circuit and 3D mechanical design and fabrication:** hands-on hardware skills for UAV and sensing projects.
- **Open-source habits:** Git version control, reproducible experiment logs, code review, and documentation.
- **Career development:** graduate school preparation, fellowship applications, and academic communication.

Students contribute to projects in **environmental UAV navigation** (RSL) and **computational microscopy imaging** (CIRL). Several mentees have presented research results at internal symposia and gone on to graduate programs.
