**Jul 2013 – May 2021** &middot; ~8 years.

I taught **programming and robotics** to gifted middle-school students at one of the most selective NODET schools.

**Programming track:**
- Languages: Python, C, C++, Qt, Small Basic, Scratch.
- Concepts: algorithms and flowcharts, complexity, data structures, object-oriented programming.
- Hosted a **graph theory workshop**.
- Coached student teams to compete in national and international programming and robotics competitions.

**Robotics track:**
- Foundations of analog and digital circuit design.
- Building and programming robots from scratch in C with CodeVisionAVR.
- Sensor integration, line-following, soccer-bot, and rescue-bot platforms.
- Mentored students through national-level competition rounds.

The eight years at Farzanegan shaped my conviction that good teaching requires working *alongside* students on real, challenging problems. Students who started not knowing what a variable was went on to build autonomous robots and compete nationally.
