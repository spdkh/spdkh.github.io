**Jan 2018 – Aug 2019.**

**FPGA Workshop Instructor, Science Students Council, EE Dept.**

A multi-week practical workshop on FPGA design, taking participants from "what is a hardware description language?" to a fully working Verilog implementation on a Xilinx ISE flow.

**Lab Instructor & Teaching Assistant, Computational Intelligence Course**

Co-designed the lesson plan for the lab course. Helped students build neural-network and fuzzy-logic systems in MATLAB / Simulink, modeling, control, classification, regression, clustering, and back-prop-trained MLPs. Worked alongside the lecturer on a UGV control project that integrated computational intelligence with the full lesson sequence. Graded assignments, projects, and reports.
